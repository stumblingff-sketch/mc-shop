Server Rules

Please read carefully. Ignorance is not an excuse.

01

Be Respectful

Treat all players and staff with respect. Harassment, racism, sexism, and hate speech will result in an immediate ban.

02

No Hacking

Using hacked clients (Killaura, Fly, X-Ray), auto-clickers, or macros gives an unfair advantage and is strictly prohibited.

03

No Griefing

Do not destroy builds that do not belong to you. Stealing from claimed land or raiding bases in non-PvP zones is not allowed.

04

No Spamming

Keep the chat clean. Do not spam messages, use excessive caps, or flood the chat with characters.

05

No Exploits

Abusing server bugs, glitches, or duplication exploits for personal gain is forbidden. Report any bugs to staff immediately.

06

No Advertising

Do not advertise other Minecraft servers, Discord links, or websites in the global chat or via private messages.

07

PvP Rules

Do not combat log (disconnect while fighting) to avoid death. TP-Trapping (teleporting players to kill them) is not allowed.

08

Appropriate Content

Inappropriate usernames, skins, or building offensive structures/symbols will result in a ban or build removal.

09

Lag Machines

Do not build large Redstone clocks or AFK farms that intentionally cause server lag. These will be removed without refund.

10

Listen to Staff

Staff decisions are final. Do not argue with staff in public chat. If you believe a punishment was unfair, open a ticket on Discord.
