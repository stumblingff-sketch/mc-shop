Server Rules

Following these rules ensures a fair and enjoyable experience for everyone.

#1

Be Respectful

Treat all players and staff with respect. Harassment, bullying, and hate speech will not be tolerated.

#2

No Hacking or Cheating

Using hacked clients, x-ray, macros, or any unfair advantages is strictly forbidden and will result in a ban.

#3

No Griefing or Stealing

Do not destroy, deface, or steal other players' property. This includes unclaimed areas.

#4

No Spamming or Advertising

Keep the chat clean. Do not spam messages, characters, or advertise other servers or websites.

#5

No Exploits or Glitches

Abusing server bugs, glitches, or exploits for personal gain is not allowed. Report any bugs you find to staff.

#6

Keep Language Appropriate

Avoid excessive swearing, offensive topics, and NSFW content in chat and on signs.

#7

Respect Staff Decisions

Do not argue with staff members in public chat. If you have an issue, please submit a ticket or contact them privately.

#8

No Lag Machines or AFK Farms

Constructing machines or farms that cause significant server lag is prohibited. Auto-fish farms are not allowed.

#9

Inappropriate Builds/Skins

Do not create builds or use skins that are offensive, political, or NSFW in nature.

#10

Use Common Sense

If something seems like it might be against the rules, it probably is. Play fair and have fun!
